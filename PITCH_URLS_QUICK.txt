🔧 HOW TO GET TRADE ID:

1. Go to: https://localhost:3000/marketplace
2. Click on any offer
3. Click "Accept Offer"
4. You'll be redirected to: https://localhost:3000/trade/[TRADE_ID]
5. Copy the TRADE_ID from the URL
6. Use it in your pitch
